# Pickles Content Rules

Use this document to write, edit, or review articles, explainers, case studies, newsletters, social posts, landing-page copy, and interface text in the requested language. It is independent of software development: it does not require a repository, build, deployment, browser audit, agent team, or engineering documents.

User instructions, confirmed facts, author intent, and language grammar take precedence over house style. MUST is a requirement in its stated scope; DEFAULT is an overridable preference; REVIEW calls for judgment. These rules improve communication; they do not identify AI authorship or promise search rankings.

## Working method

1. Identify reader, task, format, publication channel, language, and available evidence. Use supplied context; ask only for a material missing fact. A rewrite needs the original text. A new draft can proceed with explicit gaps instead of fabricated facts.
2. Read the Copy rules below. Apply C01–C19 and C26 to general prose; C20–C25 additionally when actions, interface states, product facts, localization, or conditions are present.
3. For substantial articles or research, apply E01–E08 below. A typo or short caption does not need a research dossier.
4. Review meaning and evidence before polishing sentences. Preserve real uncertainty and the author's position. Deliver the complete requested artifact; distinguish editorial notes and unresolved factual gaps from publication-ready copy.
5. If publication is authorized, inspect the published text, links, headings, captions, and changed facts in the available publishing surface. Otherwise deliver a draft and do not claim it was published. Technical website implementation and release verification belong to the separate development standard.

## Format routing

| Format | Acceptance focus |
| --- | --- |
| Article / explainer | Answers the reader's question; coherent argument; supported facts; useful examples; limitations |
| Case study | Actual starting problem, intervention, timeframe, measured outcome and attribution; no invented client quote or causation |
| Newsletter / social post | A clear point suited to the channel; accurate excerpt and destination; no invented urgency |
| Landing page | Concrete offer, audience, evidence, conditions, and honest next action |
| Interface text | Action, state, recovery, terminology, accessibility and localization; no internal debug details |
| Edit / critique | Preserve scope and voice; show the actual issue and a proportionate correction |

## Editorial depth and publication

### E01 [MUST] Build around a real reader question
For a substantial piece, state the question it answers and the contribution beyond rearranging familiar claims. Use the amount of detail the subject needs. No universal word count, paragraph quota, mandatory FAQ, or keyword density.

**Example:** An article on choosing a CRM compares relevant workflows, migration constraints, and costs when known. It does not pad an introduction about digital transformation.

**Verify:** A reader can identify the answer and use the distinctions to make progress. A personal essay may contribute an experience or argument rather than instructions.

### E02 [MUST] Match each consequential claim to evidence
For researched material, keep a compact evidence ledger: claim, primary source or supplied record, relevant date, scope/qualification, and verification status. Read the source itself when accessible. Search snippets and other generated answers are leads, not final proof. Separate observation, quotation, interpretation, and recommendation. Recheck time-sensitive claims near publication.

**Example:** A price comparison records currency, billing period, plan, taxes when relevant, and date checked. If a page is inaccessible, omit the unverified figure or mark it unresolved.

**Verify:** Sources support the particular claim, not merely its topic. Supplied internal evidence stays in authorized storage; public drafts must not leak it. Fiction is clearly framed as fiction.

### E03 [MUST] Preserve attribution and the limits of evidence
Quote accurately and sparingly within applicable permissions; link or name the original source. Paraphrase from understanding rather than swapping synonyms. Do not transfer a vendor's claim into the author's established fact, describe correlation as causation, or turn a single case into a universal result.

**Example:** “The vendor reports…” remains attributed unless independently verified. A hypothetical calculation states its assumptions.

**Verify:** Quotes match the source; numbers retain denominators, timeframe and units; conflicting credible evidence is represented where material.

### E04 [DEFAULT] Let structure follow the argument
Use an outline when the piece needs one. Each section advances the answer, adds evidence, explains a tradeoff, or supplies a necessary example. Titles describe the actual content. Avoid mechanically repeating definition → benefits → steps → FAQ → conclusion across unrelated articles.

**Example:** A troubleshooting article can begin with symptoms and branch by cause. A reported narrative may unfold chronologically.

**Verify:** Remove any section that repeats a previous point without helping the reader. Keep summaries when they help navigation through long material.

### E05 [DEFAULT] Keep voice stable and adapt tone to the situation
Use supplied samples or an established voice guide. Adjust seriousness and detail for the reader's situation. Preserve idiom, humor, first-person stance and natural variation where they belong; do not insert fake anecdotes, errors, slang, or forced jokes to appear human.

**Example:** A failure notification is direct and helpful even if the brand uses humor in campaigns. Russian grammatical dashes and accurate quotations survive stylistic cleanup.

**Verify:** Read the whole piece for consistency of person, register and terminology. A phrase blacklist only flags passages for review; it cannot certify quality or authorship.

### E06 [MUST] Make search-facing content truthful and useful
For web publication, align the title, introduction, headings, description, and actual answer. Use the audience's terminology naturally. Include meaningful original information or analysis when available; do not manufacture experience or scale out near-duplicate pages solely for search traffic. Link descriptively to relevant sources and next steps.

**Example:** Do not promise a tested product comparison if the author only read specifications. Say how the comparison was made.

**Verify:** The page delivers its title's promise and adds usable information. Exact Pickles metadata ranges and LLM endpoint requirements belong to the development SEO module when that standard is adopted; they are not Google requirements or rules for every article draft.

### E07 [MUST] Review the complete publication surface
Check claims, grammar, links, heading order, tables, captions, meaningful image descriptions, byline/date when appropriate, localization, and sensitive information. Preserve approved legal text unless changing it is in scope. Show material conditions near the decision. For publication, check that the CMS preserved the intended content.

**Example:** A corrected price must not remain outdated in a summary, image caption or call to action. Describe what an informative chart shows; decorative images do not need keyword-stuffed descriptions.

**Verify:** Record actual checks and unresolved publication blockers. A text-only task does not require running a software test suite.

### E08 [MUST] Give maintained content an owner and update trigger
For reusable or recurring publication, identify the responsible editor and triggers such as a product change, expired offer, new evidence, or a broken source. Correct substantive errors transparently according to the publication's policy. Do not change the date simply to make unchanged material look fresh.

**Example:** A comparison is reviewed when a compared product changes its pricing. A personal archival essay can remain dated historical material.

**Verify:** Handoff states draft/published status, unresolved facts, owner, and next action where applicable. No background monitoring or automatic publishing is implied.

## Editorial review examples

Fictional examples, not claims about Pickles or any client:

- «Исследования показывают, что наш подход повышает конверсию на 40%» → remove the figure unless a relevant study or project record supports it. Do not replace it with another invented number.
- «Это не просто CRM, это революция в продажах» → if confirmed: «В CRM можно назначить ответственного за заявку и увидеть историю переписки».
- “I think we need another trial. The early results are promising, highlighting our commitment to excellence.” → “I think we need another trial. The early results are promising.” Keep uncertainty; remove the unsupported flourish.
- «Наша задача — сделать заказ понятным» → preserve the grammatical dash. Edit the sentence only if its meaning is unnecessary or unclear.

## Completion record

For substantial work, briefly state: delivered format and status; evidence checked; material edits; unresolved facts or publication conditions. Keep the working evidence ledger outside the final prose unless the user requested a research report. For a small edit, the corrected text alone can be sufficient.
